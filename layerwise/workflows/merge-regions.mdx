---
title: "Merge Region Renders"
description: "Blend a re-rendered region of your image into the existing render element layers, cropped by a mask."
---

Sometimes only part of the image changes: a new sofa, a fixed material, a noisy corner. Instead of re-rendering the full frame, you render just that **region** with the same render elements. **Merge as regions** puts each region render on top of its element's existing layer, cut out by a mask, so the rest of the image stays as it was.

## What you need

- The PSD with its render element layers, open in Photoshop.
- The region renders: the same elements, rendered at the **full image size** with only the region filled in (the usual output of a region render in V-Ray, Corona or FStorm).
- Ideally a **mask** for the region, typically the Alpha pass of the region render. Its white area is what gets merged.

## Merge the regions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Update renders">
    With the PSD open, press **Update renders** on Home and pick the region renders, as in [Update renders](/layerwise/workflows/update-renders). Include the region's Alpha file in your pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the pairing">
    In the plan, make sure each layer has its region render and turn off the ones you don't want to merge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Merge as regions">
    The **Merge as regions** dialog asks for a **Region mask**. Choose the region's Alpha file from the list (any of the picked files can be used).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Merge">
    Press **Merge regions**. Each layer is updated as its own undo step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Choosing the mask that crops the regions">
  <img src="/images/layerwise/lw_region_dialog.webp" alt="The Region mask dialog listing the picked render files" />
</Frame>

If you choose **None, merge uncropped**, LayerWise asks you to confirm: without a mask the region renders are merged whole, and their black areas cover the old image.

## What happens

- The mask file becomes a channel named `regionAlphaMask` in the document (it stays there, so you can reuse it as a selection).
- For each layer, the region render is placed above it, masked by the region channel, and both are combined into a single smart object. Inside it you find the old render with the region on top.
- The layer's name, opacity, blend mode, visibility, mask, smart filters and layer styles are moved onto the new smart object, so it looks and behaves like before.

The **Resize** and **mask** options of the plan work the same as in a normal update. *Keep each layer's current size and position* is not used when merging regions.

<Frame caption="Each merged layer holds the old render with the masked region on top">
  <img src="/images/layerwise/lw_region_result.webp" alt="The Layers panel after merging regions, and the contents of a merged smart object" />

<Tip>
Merging several regions over time is fine: each merge adds a new region on top inside the smart object, and the older regions stay inside.
</Tip>
