## Requirements

- **Adobe Photoshop 2025 (26.0)** or newer
- **Windows 10/11** or **macOS**
- A free **Pulze account** (you can create one while signing in)
- An internet connection for the first sign-in. After that, LayerWise works offline.

<Info>
On Windows, the LayerWise panel is drawn with Microsoft Edge WebView2, which comes with Windows 10 and 11. If the panel stays blank, see the [FAQ](/layerwise/faq#the-panel-is-blank).
</Info>

### Supported renders

- **Render engines:** V-Ray, Corona and FStorm
- **File formats:** PNG, JPEG, TIFF, TGA and EXR, including [multichannel EXR](/layerwise/workflows/multichannel-exr)

## Install LayerWise

LayerWise comes as a single `.ccx` file, the standard package for Photoshop plugins. Creative Cloud installs it for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download">
    Download the LayerWise `.ccx` file from [pulze.io](https://pulze.io).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install">
    Double-click the file. Creative Cloud opens and asks you to confirm the install. It may warn that the plugin comes from an unverified developer; confirm to continue. LayerWise is installed into Photoshop on this computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    In Photoshop, open **Plugins → Pulze → LayerWise**. You can dock the panel anywhere, like any other Photoshop panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Opening the panel from Photoshop's Plugins menu">
  <img src="/images/layerwise/lw_install_plugins_menu.webp" alt="Photoshop's Plugins menu with Pulze and its LayerWise entry highlighted" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
You can find LayerWise under **Plugins → Manage Plugins…** in Photoshop, where it's listed as **Pulze**. That's also where you disable or uninstall it.
</Tip>

## Sign in

The first time you open the panel, LayerWise asks you to sign in with your Pulze account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Sign in">
    LayerWise shows a short code and opens a browser tab for you. If no tab opens, press **Open browser**, or open the link shown in the panel yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the code">
    Check that the code in the browser matches the one in the panel, then sign in (or create a free account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The panel notices on its own and opens the Home screen. If the code expires before you finish, press **Get a new code**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Signing in: confirm the code shown in the panel in your browser">
  <img src="/images/layerwise/lw_install_signin.webp" alt="The LayerWise sign-in screen with a device code and an Open browser button" />
</Frame>

You stay signed in until you sign out from the menu or from [Settings](/layerwise/settings#account). LayerWise never sees or stores your password.

<Note>
LayerWise stores its settings, templates and sign-in per Photoshop version. After upgrading to a new major Photoshop version you sign in again, and your own templates need to be [exported and imported](/layerwise/templates#move-templates-to-another-computer-or-photoshop-version) (or kept in the [cloud](/layerwise/cloud-templates)).
</Note>

## Updates

LayerWise checks once per launch whether a newer version exists and shows a green banner with **What's new** when it does. **Get the update** takes you to the download; install the new `.ccx` the same way, and your settings and templates stay. See [Settings → Check for updates](/layerwise/settings#about-and-updates).
