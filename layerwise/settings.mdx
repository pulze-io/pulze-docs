## Layers

<ResponseField name="Layer transforms in new documents" type="Reset to 100% | Leave as placed" default="Reset to 100%">
  With **Reset to 100%**, [Create new document](/layerwise/workflows/create-document) bakes every placed smart object at the end so its scale reads 100%. How it looks doesn't change. With **Leave as placed**, smart objects keep the scale they were placed at.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Created layers are" type="Smart objects | Linked smart objects" default="Smart objects">
  **Smart objects** store the render inside the PSD. **Linked smart objects** link to the render file on disk, keeping the PSD small, but the files must stay where they are.

  Baking embeds linked smart objects, so new documents still embed them while *Reset to 100%* is on. The screen reminds you of this.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Name created layers after the" type="File name | Element name" default="File name">
  **File name** keeps Photoshop's default name, for example `cam01.Reflection.png`. **Element name** names them after the element, for example `Reflection`. Multimatte and luma layers always keep their file name, since it holds the [nametags](/layerwise/masks#nametags).
</ResponseField>

## Masks

<ResponseField name="Multimatte channels to extract" type="R, G, B" default="All on">
  Which channels of a multimatte become masks. Turn one off if you never use it. The other channels keep their tags.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Nametag wildcard" type="# | $ | & | @ | %" default="#">
  The character that starts a [nametag](/layerwise/masks#nametags) in multimatte file names and names the mask targets (`#Wood`, `$Wood`…). The example below the picker shows the result.
</ResponseField>

**Manage render elements** opens the [Render elements](/layerwise/render-elements) list.

## AI assistant (MCP)

Shows whether the AI assistant bridge is **Off**, **On, waiting for your AI app**, or **Connected**. **Set up** opens the [AI assistant](/layerwise/ai-assistant) page.

## Account

Shows the Pulze account you're signed in with, and **Sign out**.

## About and updates

The LayerWise version, and the versions of Photoshop and UXP it's running in.

- **Check for updates** asks right away whether a newer version is available. When one is, the button becomes **Update to *version*** and shows what's new.
- Settings, templates and element lists are stored **per Photoshop version**. [Export your templates](/layerwise/templates#move-templates-to-another-computer-or-photoshop-version) to move them to a newer Photoshop.
