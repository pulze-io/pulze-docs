LayerWise is a Photoshop plugin for architectural visualization post-production. You render your scene with its render elements (passes such as Reflection, Lighting, ZDepth or MultiMatte) and LayerWise brings them into Photoshop for you: sorted into groups, set to the right blend modes, and with masks already cut from your multimatte and alpha passes.

When the client asks for changes and you render again, LayerWise swaps the new files into the same PSD. Your adjustment layers, masks, smart filters and layer styles stay where they are.

## What it's good for

- **Starting a post-production PSD in seconds.** Pick your render output folder and a template, and LayerWise builds the whole layer structure. No more dragging 20 passes into Photoshop by hand and setting each one to Linear Dodge.
- **Re-renders without redoing your work.** Update a finished PSD with tonight's renders. Each render element layer gets its new file, and everything you built on top survives.
- **Masks from your mattes.** MultiMatte, CMasking_Mask and luminance passes such as Alpha or ZDepth become layer masks on groups, ready for selective adjustments.
- **Region renders.** Merge a re-rendered region back into the full image, cropped by its alpha.
- **Your studio's structure, every time.** Save the group structure you like as a template and share it with your team, or keep it in the cloud.
- **Tedious fixes in one click.** Reset layer transforms, collapse groups, batch-convert to smart objects and more.
- **AI assistants.** Optionally let Claude or another AI app drive Photoshop through LayerWise.

LayerWise is the successor of **Pulze Post Manager**. See [Migrating from Post Manager](/layerwise/migrating-from-post-manager) if you used it.
