Project Dream puts the leading image, video, 3D and audio AI models in one app, built for architectural visualization and 3D work. Grab your viewport or drop in a render, describe what you want, and press **Dream**.

<Frame caption="Pick a mode, set it up, press Dream. Results land in your project.">

</Frame>

## What it's good for

- **Viewport to photoreal image.** Turn a clay or work-in-progress viewport from 3ds Max, SketchUp, Revit and others into a finished-looking image.
- **Editing with words.** "Make it winter", "add people on the terrace", "swap the sofa for leather". Paint a mask or draw on the image to show where.
- **Upscaling.** Enlarge renders and add believable detail with Pulze and Magnific upscalers.
- **Realistic people.** Turn stiff 3D characters into photo-grade humans with the Character Enhancer.
- **Video.** Animate a still, upscale footage to 4K, or restyle an existing clip.
- **3D and sound.** Build a 3D model from one image, add sound effects to a video, or compose music.
- **Repeatable pipelines.** Chain steps into a [Flow Graph](/project-dream/workflows/flow-graph), or lay out and iterate on a shared [Canvas](/project-dream/workflows/canvas).

## Where you can use it

| | |
|---|---|
| **Web app** | [dream.pulze.io](https://dream.pulze.io) in any browser, also on phones and tablets |
| **Desktop app** | Windows app that connects to your 3D apps and Photoshop |
| **Plugins** | 3ds Max, Maya, Revit, Rhino, SketchUp, Cinema 4D, Blender and Photoshop |
| **Claude** | Generate from a Claude chat with the [Claude connector](/project-dream/integrations/claude-connector) |
