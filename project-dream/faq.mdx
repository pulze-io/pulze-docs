description: "Quick answers to common Project Dream questions."
---

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Which mode should I use to turn a viewport into a photoreal image?">
Start with [Edit Image](/project-dream/modes/edit-image) and an instruction like *"Make this a photorealistic render, keep the architecture exactly the same."* For Pulze-model results that are free with a license, use [Image to Image](/project-dream/modes/text-and-image-to-image#image-to-image).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does the model change my building?">
Say what must stay: *"keep the architecture, camera and proportions exactly the same."* Use a [mask](/project-dream/modes/edit-image#mask-only-change-this-region) to limit the edit to one area.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I lose credits if a job fails?">
No. Press **Refund** on the failed job, or **Retry** it for free. See [Refunds](/project-dream/account/credits-and-licenses#refunds).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why are my results watermarked?">
You're on the free trial and used a Pulze model. A Pro license removes the watermark.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do my inputs go when I use a third-party model?">
To that model's maker, to run the job. Dream asks for consent the first time. To stay on Pulze models only, turn on **Hide 3rd party models and services** in [Settings](/project-dream/account/settings).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The Grab viewport button is missing.">
Grabbing needs the desktop app and the plugin in your 3D app. See [3D app plugins](/project-dream/integrations/plugins) and [Plugin not installed](/project-dream/troubleshooting/plugin-not-installed).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Dream on a Mac or a phone?">
Yes, in the browser at [dream.pulze.io](https://dream.pulze.io). The desktop app and 3D plugins are Windows-only. Flow Graph and Canvas need a screen at least 1024 px wide.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is my work public?">
No. Results only appear in the Showcase if you choose **Add to public showcase**.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
