The Project Dream plugin for Photoshop brings the main modes into a Photoshop panel and works on your document directly.

## Setup

- Photoshop 2023 or newer (24.4+). The plugin comes with the [desktop app installer](/project-dream/getting-started/installation).
- Open it from **Plugins → Project Dream**.
- Sign in: the panel shows a code and opens your browser. Confirm it there.

## Selection in, layer out

1. Make a **marquee selection** of the area to change. No selection = the whole canvas.
2. Choose a mode: **Instruct** (Edit Image), **Upscale**, **Prompt** (Text to Image) or **Generate** (Image to Image).
3. Press Dream. Dream sends the selection with a little padding around it.
4. The result is placed back **exactly in your selection**, clipped to its shape, as a new layer.

<Frame caption="Selection → result placed back as a smart object">
  <img src="https://placehold.co/1424x600?text=pd_ps_selection_result" alt="Three frames: a selection around an empty terrace, the Dream result with furniture, and the Layers panel showing a new smart object 'Instruct - add outdoor furniture' clipped to the selection" />

- **Smart object** (on by default) puts all versions into one smart object; turn it off to get one layer per result.
- Several results? They're all placed; toggle visibility to choose.
- Layers are named after the mode and prompt.

## With the desktop app

The desktop app can also **Grab Document** or **Grab Selection** from Photoshop, and any result in Dream has **Send to Photoshop** to place it in the open document.

<Note>
The Photoshop plugin and the desktop app on the same computer share one license seat.
</Note>
