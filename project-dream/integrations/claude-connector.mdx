The Project Dream connector lets Claude use Dream for you. Ask in plain words, like *"Make this render look like a winter evening and upscale it 2×"*, and Claude picks the model, tells you the price, runs the job and shows the result in the chat.

## Add it to Claude

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the connector">
    In Claude (claude.ai or Claude Desktop), open **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector** and paste:

    ```
    https://dream-mcp.pulze.io
    ```
    Or use the **Add to Claude** button in Dream's [Settings](/project-dream/account/settings) → MCP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Pulze">
    Sign in with your existing Project Dream account and allow access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Just ask">
    Start a chat and ask for an image.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On Team and Enterprise plans, an owner adds the connector once under **Organization settings → Connectors**, then members press **Connect**.

## What Claude can do

- **Generate** images from text, **edit** images, **upscale** them and make **videos**.
- **List models**, **estimate costs** and check your **credit balance** (free).
- Manage jobs: check, wait, cancel, retry, delete.

## Good to know

- You need an existing Project Dream account. Sign up in the app first.
- Jobs land in your **Playground** project and use your **personal credits**, like any other job.
- Files you attach in the chat don't reach Dream. Claude gives you an **upload link** to drop the image into instead.
- Claude tells you the price before anything expensive, like video.
