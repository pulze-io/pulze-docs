## Modes

A **mode** is what you want to do: *Text to Image*, *Edit Image*, *Creative Upscaler*, *Animate Image* and so on. You pick it on the **left rail**, grouped into Image, Video, Audio and 3D. Each mode shows only the inputs and settings it needs.

| Group | Modes |
|---|---|
| Image | [Text to Image, Image to Image](/project-dream/modes/text-and-image-to-image), [Edit Image](/project-dream/modes/edit-image), [Creative Upscaler](/project-dream/modes/creative-upscaler), [Character Enhancer](/project-dream/modes/character-enhancer) |
| Video | [Animate Image, Video Upscale, Edit Video](/project-dream/modes/video) |
| Audio | [Music Generation, Audio Generation](/project-dream/modes/3d-and-audio) |

## Models

Inside a mode you choose a **model**: Pulze's own (ClearVision, VividDreams…) or third-party ones like Nano Banana, Seedream, Kling, Veo or Magnific. Every mode has a sensible default, so you can ignore the picker until you want something specific. The picker shows each model's price, speed and what inputs it takes. See [all models](/project-dream/models).

Third-party models run on their makers' servers, so your inputs are uploaded there. Dream asks for your consent the first time. You can hide all third-party models in [Settings](/project-dream/account/settings).
</Note>

## Jobs

Pressing **Dream** creates a **job**. It moves through *Pending → Queued → Running → Saving → Completed* (or *Failed*) and shows up as a card in your feed right away, so you can keep working while it runs. A job keeps everything that made it: inputs, prompt, model and settings. That's what lets you retry it or reuse its inputs later.

## Projects

Every job lands in a **project**. The **Playground** is your scratchpad and the default target. Make projects per client or per scene, share them with your team, and move jobs between them by dragging. See [Projects](/project-dream/results/projects).

## Credits

Most jobs cost **credits**. The price is on the Dream button before you press it. Pulze's own models are free with a license (watermarked on trial). Failed jobs can be refunded. See [Credits & licenses](/project-dream/account/credits-and-licenses).

## Chaining

Results are inputs for the next step. From any result you can **Send result to** another mode (upscale it, animate it, edit it, make it 3D). When you want the same chain again and again, build it as a [Flow Graph](/project-dream/workflows/flow-graph). To compare and iterate visually, use a [Canvas](/project-dream/workflows/canvas).
