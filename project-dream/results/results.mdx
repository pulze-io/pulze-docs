## The job feed

Every job appears as a card the moment you press Dream, grouped by day. Switch between **Grid view**, **List view** and **Compact view**, and make tiles larger or smaller.

- **Search prompts** with **/**.
- **Filters:** job type, rating, *With notes*, *Unseen jobs*.
- **Select** jobs with their checkbox (Shift-click for a range), then **Delete**.

- **Status** while it runs: Pending, Queued, Running, Saving, then Completed or Failed.
- **Rating** from *Something is off* to *Awesome*. Filter by it later.
- **Add a note** to remember why you liked it.
- **Use prompt in input** / **Copy to input** reloads the prompt or all the inputs into the composer, to tweak and run again.
- **View job details:** resolution, times, credits used.
- **Prompt review:** tips on how to write the prompt better. See [Prompts](/project-dream/results/prompts).

### Failed jobs

A failed job shows its error, a free **Retry**, and **Refund**, which deletes the job and returns its credits.

## Result actions

Hover or open a result for its actions:

| Action | |
|---|---|
| **Download image** | PNG (uncompressed) or JPG (90%) |
| **Copy image to clipboard** / **Copy Image URL** | |
| **Send result to** | Edit image, Creative upscale, Simple upscale, Enhance characters, Animate Image, Image to 3D Model, Create variations… |
| **Send to canvas** / **Send to graph** | Continue on a [Canvas](/project-dream/workflows/canvas) or in a [Flow Graph](/project-dream/workflows/flow-graph) |
| **Send to Photoshop** | Places the image in the open document (desktop app + Photoshop plugin) |
| **Add to public showcase** | Share it in the [Showcase](/project-dream/results/showcase) |

Videos add **Send last frame to video input**, **Upscale video** and **Edit video**. 3D results open in a viewer with **Download model**.

## The viewer

Click a result to open it full screen. Step through results with the arrow keys, see the inputs and settings with **Show details**, and **Compare with this input** to slide between before and after. The **Inspector** lets you zoom in to check detail.

## Artifacts

Masked and drawn edits keep their in-between steps, such as the mask crop or the raw output before it was pasted back. **Show artifacts** on the card shows them. Useful when a masked edit looks off.
