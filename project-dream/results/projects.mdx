A project is a folder of jobs. Whatever project is selected when you press **Dream** is where the result lands.

## Special projects

| | |
|---|---|
| **Playground** | Your scratchpad. Jobs land here unless you pick a project. Jobs from the [Claude connector](/project-dream/integrations/claude-connector) always land here. |
| **Latest** | Your newest jobs from every project in one list. |

## Managing projects

- **New project** asks for a name. With a team selected, you choose *Team project* or *Personal project*.
- **Folders** group projects and can be nested a few levels. Drag projects into them. Deleting a folder moves its contents up one level.
- **Rename** with **F2**. Sort by name or last modified, or drag to reorder.
- The cover image is set automatically from the latest result.
- **Drag a job** onto another project to move it.

The project menu also has **Share with team**, **Mark all as seen**, **Clear project** (deletes every job in it) and **Delete project**.

## Team projects

A project shared with a team is visible to everyone in it, and their jobs show up in it too, marked *Team member's job*. See [Teams](/project-dream/account/teams).
