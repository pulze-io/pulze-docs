## Two kinds of prompt

Models want one of two styles, and the prompt block tells you which with a chip:

| Style | Used by | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Instruction style** | Edit Image, most edit models | *Replace the carpet with oak floorboards.* |
| **Descriptive style** | Text to Image, Image to Image, most video models | *A bright Scandinavian living room, oak floor, soft morning light.* |

Keep Edit Image instructions short (about 10–140 characters) and name what should stay the same.

- **Enhance prompt** rewrites your prompt with AI and shows a suggestion to **Apply**. Turn on *Auto-apply* to skip the step.
- **Describe input image** (**Ctrl + ↑**) writes a prompt from your input image.
- **Prompt examples** opens clickable building blocks for the current mode.
- **Prompt history** searches everything you've written before.

## Prompt review

After a job runs, Dream checks your prompt against what the model likes. If it can help, the job card shows **How to improve this prompt?**, sometimes with a **Suggested prompt** you can load with **Use this prompt**. A common tip: *"This model wants an instruction, not a description"*.

## Writing for architecture

- **Lock the design.** "Keep the architecture, camera and proportions exactly the same."
- **Name materials and light.** "Board-formed concrete, overcast sky, soft shadows" beats "make it nice".
- **Add life in its own step.** People, cars and planting are easier as a second edit.
- **Iterate.** Generate 2–4, rate the best, and **Copy to input** to refine it.
