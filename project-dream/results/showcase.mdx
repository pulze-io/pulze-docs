The Showcase is a gallery of results people chose to share. Open it from **Showcases** in the header.

<Frame caption="The public Showcase">
  <img src="/images/project-dream/pd_showcase.webp" alt="Showcase page with Public/Team tabs and Staff picks selected: a masonry grid of archviz images with job-type labels and staff-pick crowns" />
</Frame>

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Public** | Results anyone made public. Filter by *Staff picks* or *All public jobs*. |
| **Team** | Results your teammates added to the team showcase. Filter by team and project. |

## Share a result

On a finished job, choose **Add to public showcase** (or **Add to Team showcase** when a team is selected). A public result can be seen by anyone. **Remove from public showcase** takes it back.

<Note>
Watermarked trial results can't be made public.
</Note>
