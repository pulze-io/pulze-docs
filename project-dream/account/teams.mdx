If you belong to a Pulze team, a **Teams badge** appears in the header. Pick the team you're working for, and Dream switches to it.

<Frame caption="Selecting a team">
  <img src="/images/project-dream/pd_teams_select.webp" alt="Select team menu from the header: No team selected, and three teams each with credits, members and licenses counts" />
</Frame>

## What changes with a team selected

- **Credits:** new jobs are paid from the team's balance, which the badge shows.
- **Licenses:** the team's Pro licenses are used first, then yours.
- **New projects** ask whether they're team or personal.

## What you can share

| | Shared how |
|---|---|
| **Projects** | **Share with team**. Everyone sees and adds jobs. |
| **Canvases** | **Share with team**. Everyone can edit, live. See [Canvas](/project-dream/workflows/canvas). |
| **Flow Graphs** | **Share with team**. Read-only for others; they duplicate to run. |
| **Results** | **Add to Team showcase**. See [Showcase](/project-dream/results/showcase). |

Teams, members and credits are managed at [account.pulze.io](https://account.pulze.io).
