## Credits

Credits pay for jobs. They work across every model, in the app, the plugins and the [Claude connector](/project-dream/integrations/claude-connector).

- **You always see the price first.** The Dream button shows the cost; it turns red if you can't afford it. An infinity icon means your license covers the job.
- **Prices depend on the model and the output:** per image, per megapixel for upscales, per second for video, per model for 3D. The model picker shows each model's rate and a *Budget / Standard / Premium* tier.
- **Buying:** press **+** on the credits badge or **Get more credits**. Larger packages cost less per credit. Credits stay valid for a limited time after purchase (shown when you buy).

## What's free

With a license, **Pulze's own models** cost no credits: Text to Image, Image to Image, Creative Upscale and the Character Enhancer with ClearVision and other Pulze models. Third-party models always cost credits.

## Refunds

- A **failed** job shows **Refund**: it deletes the job and returns its credits.
- Deleting a job that is still **pending**, or finished with no output, refunds it too.
- **Retry** on a failed job is free.

## Free trial

New users can **Start free trial**: 7 days and 250 credits. During the trial:

- Pulze-model results are **watermarked**. The watermark is baked into the image and stays after you upgrade.
- One job runs at a time, and jobs may wait in a queue.
- Downloading, copying and Send to Photoshop are locked.
- Results can't be shared to the public Showcase.

## Pro licenses

A Pro license removes trial limits and makes Pulze models free. A license seat is locked to one computer or session at a time. The **Pro Licenses** badge shows your seats (*Held by this session*, *Free*, *In use elsewhere*) and lets you **Unlock** a seat to use it on another computer. Settings can release it automatically after 30 minutes idle.
