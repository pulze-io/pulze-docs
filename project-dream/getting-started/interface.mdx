1. **Header.** The logo takes you Home. The mode selector switches between **Classic**, **Flow Graph**, **Canvas** and **Showcases**. On the right: news, team, license and credit badges, and your avatar menu.
2. **Mode rail.** Pick what to do: Image, Video, Audio or 3D modes.
3. **Composer.** The inputs, prompt and settings of the current mode, stacked as blocks. Blocks are tagged *Required*, *Optional* or *Ready*.
4. **Dream dock.** Number of results, the selected model, the credit cost and the **Dream** button (**Ctrl + Enter**). If something is missing, a line above the button tells you what.
5. **Job feed.** Your results, newest first, grouped by day.
6. **Project list.** Switch the project new jobs go into.

## Home

The Home page is your starting point: credit balance, **Pick up where you left off** (recent projects, graphs and canvases), **Model Highlights**, **Start creating** tiles for every mode, community picks, news and tutorials.

## Header menus

- **Avatar menu:** theme, language, About, Changelog, [Settings](/project-dream/account/settings), Documentation, Discord, Sign out.
- **Credits badge:** your balance and plan. Click it to refresh, **+** to buy more.
- **Teams badge:** pick the team you're working for. See [Teams](/project-dream/account/teams).
- **News badge:** new models and features.

## Getting images in

Every image input accepts the same things:

- **Drag and drop** a file.
- **Paste** with **Ctrl + V** (click the input first).
- **Grab** from a connected app: **Grab viewport**, **Grab VFB**, **Grab Document**, **Grab Selection** (desktop app, see [Plugins](/project-dream/integrations/plugins)).
- **Reuse** a result: **Send result to** a mode, or **Copy to input** on a job.

## Phones and tablets

The web app works on phones and tablets. On a phone, a tab bar at the bottom holds **Home**, **Projects**, **New dream**, **Showcase** and **You**; **New dream** opens the composer as a sheet. Flow Graph and Canvas need a screen at least 1024 px wide. On a tablet, turn it sideways.
