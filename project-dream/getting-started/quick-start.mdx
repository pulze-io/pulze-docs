This walkthrough takes a work-in-progress view and turns it into a finished image.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick Edit Image">
    On the left rail, open **Image** and choose **Edit Image**. The default model, Nano Banana 2, is a good start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your image">
    Drop a screenshot or render into the input, paste it with **Ctrl + V**, or press **Grab viewport** in the desktop app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what you want">
    Write a short instruction, for example: *Make this a photorealistic render, late afternoon sun, keep the architecture exactly the same.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Dream">
    Check the cost on the button and press **Dream** (or **Ctrl + Enter**). The job appears in your feed and fills in when it's done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep going">
    On the result, open **Send result to** → **Creative upscale** to enlarge it, or **Animate Image** to make a clip.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for good results

- **Say what must stay.** "Keep the architecture and camera exactly the same" stops the model from redesigning your building.
- **One change at a time.** Chain edits instead of cramming five into one prompt.
- **Generate a few.** Set results to 2 or 4 and pick the best one.
- **Read the review.** After a job runs, Dream may suggest a better prompt. See [Prompts](/project-dream/results/prompts).
