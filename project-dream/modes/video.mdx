Three modes live in the **Video** group of the rail.

## Animate Image

Turn a still into a clip, or make a clip from text alone. Default model: Seedance 2.0 Fast. Others include Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.5, Runway Gen-4.5, Hailuo and Wan.

The model picker's **Filter by mode** decides the inputs:

| Mode | Inputs |
|---|---|
| **Text to video** | Prompt only |
| **Image to video** | A start frame |
| **First & Last frames** | Start and end frame; the clip travels between them. **Swap** flips them. |
| **Reference Images** | Up to 6 images of people, products or places to feature |

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Aspect ratio** | Taken from the image in image modes. |
| **Resolution** | e.g. 720p / 1080p. Affects the price. |
| **Clip length** | Seconds. |
| **Audio** | Native sound, on models that support it. |
| **Draft (480p preview)** | Cheap preview. Happy with it? **Finalize in 1080p** renders the same shot in full quality. |

**Describe video from input image** writes a camera-aware prompt from your frames.

**Cost:** credits **per second** of video; the rate depends on model, resolution and audio. The picker shows the rate and typical render time.

## Video Upscale

Upscale a video to 4K, double its frame rate, or slow it down.

- **Input:** MP4, up to 50 MB. Dream shows its size, length and frame rate.
- **Models:** TopazLabs Video AI (default, with presets such as Starlight and Astra), Magnific Creative / Precision, FLUX Video Upscale, FlashVSR.
- **Settings:** **Upscale ratio**, **Frame interpolation**, **Slowmo** (keeps the frame rate, stretches the duration), **Creativity**, **Sharpness**.
- **Cost:** per second of video.

## Edit Video

Restyle or change an existing video: new season, new materials, new lighting, add a character.

- **Input video:** up to 1920 px wide (larger videos are scaled down).
- Depending on the model, also a **Re-styled first frame** (edit frame one with [Edit Image](/project-dream/modes/edit-image) first, and the whole clip follows), **Reference Images**, and a prompt.
- **Models:** Seedance 2.0 (default), Runway Aleph 2, Kling o3, Luma Ray, Gemini Omni Flash and more.
- **Cost:** per second of the source video.
