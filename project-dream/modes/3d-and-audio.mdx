## Image to 3D Model

Build a textured 3D model from a picture: furniture, props, products, entourage.

- **Front image** (required). Multi-view models also take **Back**, **Left** and **Right** images for a more accurate result.
- **Models:** Tripo H3.1 (default), Tripo P2, Meshy V7, Hunyuan3D, Rodin 2.5, Trellis 2 and more. The picker lists their input views and output formats.
- **Generate PBR** adds metallic, roughness and normal maps.
- Results open in a 3D viewer; **Download model** to use them in your 3D app.
- **Cost:** a fixed price per model.

## Audio Generation

Adds sound effects and Foley that match a video: footsteps, traffic, birds, water.

- **Input video:** up to 30 seconds, 50 MB.
- **Prompt (optional):** describe the sounds you want.
- **Models:** Mirelo SFX 1.6 (default), Hunyuan Video Foley, Sonilo.
- You get the video back with the new soundtrack. **Cost:** per second.

## Music Generation

Composes an original track from a description, with optional lyrics.

- **Prompt:** genre, mood, instruments, tempo.
- **Lyrics (optional):** leave empty for an instrumental.
- **Duration** on some models.
- **Models:** MiniMax Music 3 (default), Google Lyria, ElevenLabs Music.
- **Cost:** per track or per minute, depending on the model.
