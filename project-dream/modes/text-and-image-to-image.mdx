## Text to Image

Make an image from a prompt alone. Good for mood boards, materials, backgrounds and early concepts.

- **Input:** a prompt of at least 10 characters.
- **Default model:** Nano Banana 2. Others include Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 5 Pro, Flux 2 Pro Realism, Flux Krea [Dev] and Pulze's ClearVision.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Aspect ratio** | Shape of the image. Default 1:1. |
| **Refine image** | Twice the resolution. Slower, costs more. |
| **Number of results** | 1, 2 or 4, if the model supports it. |

With a Pulze model you also get a negative prompt, **Reference mood**, **Magic salt** and a seed (see below).

## Image to Image

Turn a viewport, clay render or sketch into a polished image with Pulze models, while keeping its composition. Unlike [Edit Image](/project-dream/modes/edit-image), you describe the *result*, not a change.

- **Input image:** **Connections** (fetch from a connected 3D app) or **Upload image**.
- **Prompt:** describe the finished image, at least 7 characters.
- **Models:** ClearVision (default) and the legacy VividDreams.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Creativity** | How much freedom the AI takes. Low = close to your input. |
| **Resemblance** | How strongly the input image is kept. |
| **Presets** | Ready slider combos: Touchup, Light, Default, Medium, High, Re-imagine. Save your own with **Save preset**. |
| **Refine image** | Twice the resolution. Not available with a mask. |
| **Mask** | Only change the painted area. |

## Pulze-model extras

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Reference mood** | An image whose light and mood the result borrows. Set **Reference type** (Light / Medium / Heavy) and **Influence**. Browse ready ones in the **Reference mood gallery**. |
| **Magic salt** | Adds Pulze's preset prompt enhancements. |
| **Negative prompt** | Things to avoid. |
| **Seed** | Random, or fixed to repeat a result. |

## Cost

Pulze models are free with a license and watermarked on a trial. Third-party models cost credits per image, times the number of results; **Refine image** costs extra. See [Credits](/project-dream/account/credits-and-licenses).
