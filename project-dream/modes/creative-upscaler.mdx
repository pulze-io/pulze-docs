The Creative Upscaler makes images larger and, unlike a plain resize, adds new detail: sharper materials, finer foliage, crisper edges. Use it as the last step before delivery.

## Inputs

- **Image:** up to 7680 px on the long side.
- **Prompt (optional):** describe the image to guide the new detail. Leave it empty if you like.

## Models

| Model | Good for |
|---|---|
| **ClearVision** (Pulze) | Everyday upscales. Free with a license. |
| **Magnific Creative** | Lots of added detail and texture. |
| **Magnific Precision v2** | Staying faithful to the original. The choice for "precision upscale". |
| **Clarity AI Crystal** | Clean, faithful enlargement. |

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Upscale style** | *Simple* (just bigger) or *Creative* (bigger + detail). Pulze models. |
| **Upscale ratio** | How much larger. The output size is shown. |
| **Creativity** | How much new detail may be invented. |
| **Resemblance** | How close to stay to the input. |
| **Restoration** / **Fractality** | Adds detail to flat areas. |
| **Keep colors** | Restores the original colors. |
| **Presets** | Ready combinations per model, e.g. Subtle / Default / Vivid / Wild. |

Magnific models add their own options, such as **Optimized For** (3D Renders, Nature & Landscapes, Portraits…), **HDR**, **Flavor** and **Smart grain**.

<Tip>
Too much invented detail? Lower **Creativity** and raise **Resemblance**, or switch to Magnific Precision.
</Tip>

## Cost

Priced by the size of the result (megapixels) and the model. Pulze models are free with a license.
